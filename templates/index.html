How It Works

Four simple steps to resolve a hostel complaint

1. Login

Student logs in using their roll number and password.

2. Raise Complaint

Enter room details, select a category, describe the issue and upload a photo.

3. Staff Assigned

The warden reviews the complaint and assigns maintenance staff.

4. Issue Resolved

Staff completes the work and updates the complaint as resolved.

Key Features

Everything needed for organized hostel complaint management

Digital Complaints

Students can submit complaints online instead of depending on verbal communication or paper registers.

Photo Upload

Students can attach a photo to clearly show the maintenance problem.

Complaint Tracking

Students can track the progress of their complaints at any time.

Role-Based Access

Separate access is provided for students, wardens, maintenance staff and admin.

Status History

Every complaint maintains a history of status updates from submission to resolution.

Organized Management

Wardens and administrators can monitor complaints and maintenance activities.
